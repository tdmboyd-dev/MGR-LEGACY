# MGR-Native Systems Catalog

These systems are MGR's synthesis layer: they are designed from competitor gaps plus MGR's own operating needs. Underlying primitives may be common industry concepts; the combined behavior is the MGR-native design.

## 1. TODAY / Next Action Engine
Turns the entire customer graph, tasks, conversations, deadlines, workflows and signals into a ranked action feed.

Contract:
- candidate actions
- urgency
- business value
- risk
- confidence
- effort
- dependency blockers
- owner
- due window
- explanation
- recommended action
- one-click execute / approve / snooze / delegate

## 2. Revenue Leak Scanner
Continuously identifies money falling out of the system:
- stale leads
- uncontacted inbound leads
- missed follow-ups
- stalled deals
- unsigned proposals
- no-show recovery
- unpaid invoices
- inactive customers
- renewal risk
- incomplete tax clients
- missing documents
- unfunded bank products
- unclaimed referrals
- failed automations

Each leak produces amount-at-risk, evidence, owner and recovery playbook.

## 3. Signal Graph
Unifies behavioral, transactional, conversational, operational and external signals into explainable entity features.

Examples:
- engagement momentum
- relationship strength
- purchase intent
- churn risk
- response likelihood
- urgency
- office health
- compliance risk

## 4. Object Forge
Lets MGR create Salesforce-style custom domain objects without code while keeping first-class validation, permissions, relationships, workflows, analytics and agent actions.

## 5. Comms Command
One conversation brain across providers/channels:
- unified thread
- contact identity resolution
- templates/sequences
- AI summaries
- intent/sentiment/commitment extraction
- consent and quiet-hour enforcement
- deliverability
- next action
- escalation
- agent assist
- full audit

## 6. Deal Room
A living opportunity workspace combining:
- stakeholders
- communications
- tasks
- docs/proposals
- approvals
- pricing
- timeline
- objections
- commitments
- risk
- forecast
- next actions
- collaboration

## 7. Forecast Brain
Forecasts using stage data plus behavior, activity, conversation, seasonality, capacity and historical patterns. Every forecast must expose drivers and uncertainty rather than only a number.

## 8. Deal Coach
Detects deal weaknesses and proposes specific interventions based on missing stakeholders, inactivity, objections, next-step gaps, probability drift and historical patterns.

## 9. Service Brain
Operational intelligence for post-sale/service work:
- case triage
- SLA risk
- customer health
- escalation
- suggested resolution
- root-cause grouping
- recurring issue detection

## 10. Growth Loop
Finds repeatable growth mechanisms:
- referral opportunities
- cross-sell/upsell
- reactivation
- review requests
- audience/segment opportunities
- high-performing sequences
- campaign-to-revenue patterns

## 11. Data Medic
Continuously checks and repairs CRM data quality:
- duplicates
- conflicting identities
- invalid fields
- missing ownership
- stale records
- broken relationships
- lifecycle contradictions
- suspicious imports
- merge suggestions
- lineage and reversible fixes

## 12. Workflow Simulator
Runs a proposed automation against historical/synthetic events before activation and shows:
- paths taken
- messages/actions that would fire
- projected volume/cost
- permission failures
- loops
- dead ends
- unintended matches

## 13. Automation Conflict Detector
Builds an interaction graph across workflows and warns about:
- duplicate sends
- contradictory updates
- recursive triggers
- race conditions
- overlapping audiences
- competing ownership changes
- schedule collisions
- cost explosions

## 14. Automation Self-Audit
Every active workflow gets ongoing health scoring:
- success/failure rate
- retries
- latency
- cost
- conversion contribution
- stale branches
- provider problems
- policy violations
- abnormal behavior

## 15. Universal Command Layer
Natural-language and structured command interface over authorized MGR actions:
- query
- create/update
- bulk operations
- build reports
- build workflows
- simulate
- explain
- execute with approval gates

The command layer never bypasses policy, permissions, idempotency, or audit.

## Native design principle

Do not chase "more buttons." The 10x target is:
1. unify fragmented context,
2. make the system explain what matters,
3. simulate before acting,
4. detect what is leaking/broken,
5. make actions executable,
6. keep every action governed and auditable.
